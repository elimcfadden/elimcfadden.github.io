About me

Since I can remember, basketball has been my favorite sport. Much of my childhood was spent on the court or in the driveway, playing competitively with friends or just shooting hoops. I’ve always been a big Denver Nuggets fan and try to watch as many games as I can. Some of my favorite players over the years have been Carmelo Anthony, Kenneth Faried, Ty Lawson, Jamal Murray, and, obviously, Nikola Jokić. I love talking basketball, whether the conversation stays friendly or not.

I graduated from Utah State University’s Jon M. Huntsman School of Business with a B.S. in Information Systems, an emphasis in Data Engineering, and a minor in Data Analytics. Working with data turned out to be something I genuinely enjoy, and the NBA is my favorite thing to point it at.

This site is my way of bringing these two passions together and getting my opinions out there. I hope you enjoy the articles, and if you disagree, send me a message below.

What Shattered Backboard Analytics is

Shattered Backboard Analytics is an NBA-only analysis site. I dig into the data, build the visualizations, and write up what they actually show. Pieces run when the analysis holds up, not on a schedule.
