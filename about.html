About

An NBA-only writing and visualization project, built on the idea that a bold take should come with the chart that backs it.

Who is behind it

My name is Eli McFadden and I work with data. Pipelines, warehouses, dashboards, and the analysis that sits on top of them. I graduated from Utah State University in December 2025 with a B.S. in Information Systems, a Data Engineering emphasis, and a minor in Data Analytics.

Most of my work is the unglamorous kind. Modeling a warehouse in Snowflake and dbt, automating a process that used to be manual cleanup, building the dashboard that finally puts a number in front of the person who can act on it. Shattered Backboard Analytics is that same work pointed at the thing I actually argue about with my friends.

I'm open to analyst roles, in sports or out of it. The domain matters less to me than the problem being real.

What Shattered Backboard Analytics is

Shattered Backboard Analytics is an NBA-only visualization and opinion site built on analysis. The idea is simple: share takes that might sound bold at first, but shouldn't be once the data is on the table.

Everything here is backed by visualizations I create, with article-style breakdowns that explain what the charts are actually saying. The charts are built in Tableau right now, with SQL, Python, and Power BI work showing up as the analysis gets deeper. Pieces run when the analysis holds up, not on a schedule.
